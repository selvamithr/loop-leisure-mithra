The Story Behind Loop & Leisure

Meet Mithra,
Our Founder

I always wanted to gift the people who mean the world to me something that felt truly personal — something more than something picked from a shelf. I wanted to give them something where I could put a little piece of my soul into what I was making, something that would make them feel special.

That was when crochet caught my eye. I was fascinated by the idea that a simple strand of yarn could slowly turn into almost anything I imagined. The more I learned, the more I wanted to create. I started making different little things as gifts for the people I loved, and every piece became more than just a craft — it became a way of expressing feelings that were sometimes difficult to put into words.

Somewhere along the way, those little gifts became something more. While I was in college, I started taking small crochet orders and used the money I earned as my pocket money. What began as something I did simply because I loved creating slowly turned into a little business of my own. And honestly, every order felt like a small win — not because I had simply sold something, but because someone had chosen something I had made with my own hands.

I knew that every stitch had taken time, patience and care, and that the piece I was making would eventually become part of someone else's story. Even today, whether I'm making a bouquet, a keychain, a teddy bear, a laptop sleeve, or something completely custom, I still begin exactly the same way — with the hope that when someone holds it, they won't just see handmade crochet. They'll feel the love, care and soul that went into creating it.

Made With Intention

Every piece at Loop & Leisure begins with a feeling, not a factory. Each stitch is placed with patience and care. There is no rushing, no cutting corners — only the quiet, deliberate work of someone who genuinely loves what she makes.

More Than Handmade

Loop & Leisure exists because mass-produced products can't carry the feeling that a handmade gift can. These aren't just items to buy — they are small expressions of love, crafted specifically to be given to people who matter. Every creation is designed to become a memory.

Made For Your People

The best gifts are the ones that make someone feel truly seen. Everything here is made for people who care enough to give something thoughtful — a birthday bouquet, a custom keychain, a little something that says "I made this thinking of you."
